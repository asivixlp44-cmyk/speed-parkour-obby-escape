Description
Run and get faster! Every step you take gives you +Speed, and every level makes you run faster. Climb the red walls, swing on poles over the lava, wall run along the blue glass, Super Jump over spike pits, slide under the walls, dodge the crushers and jump between laser pillars. Every stage ends with Wins pads: grab the yellow one, or jump up to the purple one for double. Train on treadmills, buy faster shoes in the WINS SHOP, unlock trails and rebirth for bigger multipliers (no in-app purchases, everything costs Wins!). Beat all 9 stages and ESCAPE the obby with your friends!
